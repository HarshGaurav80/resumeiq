Job Title: Data Analyst (Entry Level)
Company: Northwind Analytics (fictional)
Location: Pune / Hybrid

About the role
We are hiring a Data Analyst to join our Business Intelligence team. You will turn raw data into clear insights that help product, marketing and operations teams make decisions.

Responsibilities
- Write efficient SQL queries to extract, clean and analyze data from PostgreSQL and BigQuery.
- Build and maintain interactive dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and statistical analysis (A/B testing, hypothesis testing, regression analysis).
- Automate recurring reports using Python (Pandas, NumPy).
- Present findings to stakeholders and translate business questions into analysis.
- Work with data engineers on ETL pipelines and data quality checks.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics or a related field.
- Strong SQL and Advanced Excel skills (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP).
- Experience with Python for data analysis (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib).
- Hands-on experience with Power BI or Tableau.
- Good understanding of statistics and data visualization best practices.
- Excellent communication and problem solving skills.

Nice to have
- Experience with Google BigQuery, Looker or Airflow.
- Knowledge of Machine Learning basics.
- Google Data Analytics or Microsoft PL-300 certification.
